🐰 Privacy, in plain words

What we collect. Only your email address, the date and time you signed in, the version of the consent text you agreed to, and the website address you used. We do not ask for your name, and we do not use ads or tracking cookies.

Why. To know who is playing, and to send you occasional news about Hangul Hop (new levels, tips). You can say stop at any time and we will remove you.

Where it is kept. In a private Google Sheet owned by the Hangul Hop maker, which means Google processes it for us. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Your game progress (stars, scores, key practice) stays only on your own device, in your browser's storage. It is not uploaded and not linked to your email.

Who can sign in. You must be 13 or older. If you are younger, please ask a parent or guardian first.

Your choices. You can ask to see, correct or delete your email at any time: contact the person who shared this game with you. Using “not you?” in the game only signs you out on this device.
